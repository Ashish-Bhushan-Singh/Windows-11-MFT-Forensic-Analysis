
# Digital Forensics – Legal and Ethical Disclaimer

## 1. Purpose

This repository contains educational materials and practical exercises related to Windows digital forensics.

The project demonstrates the examination of the NTFS Master File Table (MFT) using Autopsy and MFT Explorer.

## 2. Authorized Use Only

All forensic examinations must be conducted on systems and storage devices that you own or have explicit permission to investigate.

Unauthorized access to computer systems or private information is prohibited.

## 3. Evidence Preservation

Digital forensic investigations should follow appropriate evidence-handling procedures.

Important principles include:

- Preserve original evidence.
- Work with forensic copies whenever possible.
- Document investigation procedures.
- Record file hashes when appropriate.
- Maintain evidence integrity.
- Keep accurate investigation notes.

## 4. Privacy Protection

Forensic evidence may contain sensitive personal information.

Before publishing investigation materials:

- Remove personal usernames.
- Hide private file paths.
- Remove personal documents.
- Never publish passwords.
- Avoid exposing private information.
- Do not upload complete disk images.
- Do not publish raw MFT files from personal computers.

## 5. Educational Purpose

The information in this repository is intended for:

- Digital forensics education.
- NTFS file system learning.
- Understanding MFT structures.
- Learning forensic investigation tools.
- Developing practical cybersecurity skills.

## 6. Accuracy and Limitations

The findings documented in this repository reflect the observations made during the practical investigation.

They should not be interpreted as proof of criminal activity or malicious behavior.

Additional evidence and validation may be required to support forensic conclusions.

## 7. Responsibility

The author is responsible for ensuring that all forensic activities are conducted legally and ethically.

Users of this educational material must follow applicable laws and organizational policies.

## 8. Final Statement

This repository is maintained for educational and professional development in digital forensics.

The goal is to develop practical knowledge of Windows file systems and forensic investigation techniques.
